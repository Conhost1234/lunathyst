# Information:
Lunathyst is a python-centric linux distro with a very modular and simple userland. Our goal is to make a good linux distro for everyone, even programming beginners. The desktops are fully modular, and a usable and working desktop can be written in under 10 lines and half a kilobyte in size
versioning system: major.minor.buildupdate
a major version of the os will be released every few weeks to every few months
